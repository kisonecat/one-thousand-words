A Picture Is Worth 1,000 Words

(actually just a few hundred words are enough)

Downloading models (only the first time)

    What is this?

    The Photo to words panel takes a photo and turns it into a block of text: a short story that reads like any other. Paste that block of text into the Words to photo panel, and the photo comes back out of it. The picture is (somehow!) encoded in the words themselves. After all, a picture is worth a thousand words.

    How it works

    A neural image codec (TiTok) squeezes the photo into 64 numbers, 768 bits in all. A small vision model (Florence-2) describes the photo, and a small language model (SmolLM2) opens a story about it. From then on the photo's bits, rather than chance, decide each word it picks, using arithmetic coding. To get the photo back, the same model rereads the story and works out which bits forced each word.

    Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. The models download once (about 430 MB to turn a photo into words, 250 MB to turn words back into a photo) and are cached. Change a single word and the photo is lost, so copy the story exactly.
